Circuit Bloom: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Developer: OURANOS Mobile
Contact: ouranosmobile.support@gmail.com

The short version

Circuit Bloom does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. We have no servers. The game works offline.

What stays on your device

Your game progress: completed levels, stars, Bloom Energy, garden restorations and your settings (sound volumes, reduced motion and larger text). It is saved in the game's own storage on your device so you can continue where you left off.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself, for example through your device's own backup, which is governed by Apple's policies. Deleting the game deletes it.

Purchases

If you buy the optional in-app purchase, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. The game only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can unlock it on your device and restore it on your other devices.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No contact with any server operated by us.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We don't add you to any mailing list and we don't share your message with anyone. Our support mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), so Google's privacy policy also applies to messages sent to it. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed to help you; you can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Children

Circuit Bloom is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes, such as adding any data collection, will be made before the app version that needs them and reflected in the App Store privacy details.
