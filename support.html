Where is my progress saved?

On your device, in the game's own storage. There are no accounts, so progress doesn't move between devices by itself. If you delete the game, its progress is deleted too.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Apple handles the purchase; we never see your payment details.

Can I turn off motion or make the text bigger?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Reduced motion to stop drifting, particles and animated light, and turn on Larger text to make all text 20% bigger.

I can't hear any sound

The game follows your iPhone's Ring/Silent switch: in silent mode it stays quiet. You can also set music, effects and ambience volumes in Settings.
